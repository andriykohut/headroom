The mark

One mark for three places that want different things: a masked, parallaxed launcher icon; a monochrome icon recoloured to the wallpaper; and a 24dp white silhouette in the status bar, where only the alpha channel survives. Six directions were drawn; this is the decision row, 48dp in colour and 24dp as the silhouette, with no help given to any of them.

1. Disc and slit
The meter's threshold notch, cut through a solid disc. The sliver above is the headroom.
2. Cut bar
The notch alone. One capsule, severed where the warning line is; no container, no fill.
3. Archway
Clearance under a limit: a span with an arch cut from below. The hole is the headroom; the mass is what is used.
4. Ceiling
A horizon line with a head beneath it. The gap between them is the room above.
5. Hourglass, cut
Capacity that drains and comes back when the window flips. The waist is the threshold gap.
6. Monogram
A geometric H with its crossbar raised to the slit's height. No metering imagery at all.

Verdict

The row is judged at 24dp, because that is where a notification icon lives and where marks die. The cut bar collapses into a broken toggle. The ceiling becomes a shelf with a ball under it. The hourglass is beautiful at launcher size and unusable in the status bar, where Android already draws an hourglass for pending states. The monogram is crisp until a circle mask turns an H into a helipad. The archway is the one real contender: its meaning is the word itself, clearance under a limit, and it reads at every size. It loses to the disc at launcher size: a block with a hole is a picture of a tunnel, and it fights the masks, sitting as a box inside a squircle and a box inside a circle, while the disc is the only shape that is at home under all four. The disc is also the only mark whose gap is literally the notch on the bars, so icon and screen are one system rather than two. It says less than the archway, and that is the trade: quieter, more ownable, more at home. The recommendation is the disc; the choice is yours, and the archway is buildable from the path data below.

The disc, in context

A disc, cut once by a thin slit near the top. The body below the slit is what you have used; the slit is the warning line from the meter, the same notch that cuts through every bar on the Usage screen; the sliver above it is the headroom. Two paths and one gap, so the silhouette, the single-colour themed icon and the full-colour launcher icon are the same shape, not three interpretations of it.

It is not a battery, not a gauge, not a pie and not a bar in a box. The slit is thin and off-centre, which keeps it clear of the no-entry sign even when a wallpaper tints it red.
